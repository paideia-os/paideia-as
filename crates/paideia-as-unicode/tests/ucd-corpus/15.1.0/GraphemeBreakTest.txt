# Format per UAX #29 §3.1:
#   ÷ (U+00F7) = break opportunity at the preceding byte offset
#   × (U+00D7) = no-break marker (not a boundary)
#   Code points are hexadecimal.
#
# Hand-crafted smoke rows (3 of them) exercise GB1/GB2 (SOT/EOT),
# GB3 (CR × LF fuses), and GB4 (control character breaks).
#
÷ 0020 ÷ 0020 ÷	# ÷ [0.2] SPACE (Other) ÷ [999.0] SPACE (Other) ÷ [0.3]
÷ 000D × 000A ÷	# ÷ [0.2] <CR> (CR) × [3.0] <LF> (LF) ÷ [0.3]
÷ 0061 ÷ 0007 ÷	# ÷ [0.2] LATIN SMALL LETTER A (Other) ÷ [5.0] <BEL> (Control) ÷ [0.3]
