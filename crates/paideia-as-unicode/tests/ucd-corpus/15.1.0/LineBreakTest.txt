# Format per UAX #14 §7:
#   × (U+00D7) = no break at the preceding byte offset
#   ÷ (U+00F7) = break opportunity at the preceding byte offset
#   Rows always start with × (LB2 — no break at start of text).
#   Code points are hexadecimal.
#
# Hand-crafted smoke rows (3 of them) exercise:
#   1. Two adjacent letters: no interior break (LB18 catches only EOT).
#   2. Letter-space-letter: allowed break after the space (LB18 / SP).
#   3. Mandatory break after LF (LB5).
#
× 0061 × 0062 ÷	# × [0.3] LATIN SMALL LETTER A (AL) × [28.0] LATIN SMALL LETTER B (AL) ÷ [0.3]
× 0061 × 0020 ÷ 0062 ÷	# × [0.3] LATIN SMALL LETTER A (AL) × [7.01] SPACE (SP) ÷ [18.0] LATIN SMALL LETTER B (AL) ÷ [0.3]
× 0061 × 000A ÷ 0062 ÷	# × [0.3] LATIN SMALL LETTER A (AL) × [6.0] <LF> (LF) ÷ [5.0] LATIN SMALL LETTER B (AL) ÷ [0.3]
